Orchestration marks

These replace the ↳ glyph and the related-row left bar. Each mark sits at the end of the name line, away from the status light. Transmitting: an orchestrator, with waves on both sides of the antenna, like a radio tower. Receiving: a child, with a Wi-Fi-style fan that opens upward from the antenna. At rest both are muted and still. Hovering or selecting a session turns its relatives' marks accent-colored and pulses their waves, outward for the orchestrator and inward for children. Sidebar: at rest, then with the orchestrator selected

A child whose orchestrator has closed keeps a dashed, muted fan with no relatives to light up. A taken-over child loses its mark and becomes an ordinary session. Accessible names still carry the relationship in words: "Orchestrator · 3 children" and "Child of foom".
