Split and Grow pads

The tile panel replaces the four Split/Grow rows with two directional pads. Right-clicking empty space opens a short menu: the Split pad, New shell here, and Close space. Hover a dimmed arrow to see why it is refused.

Split ← ↑ → ↓Splits this tile and puts the new space on that side. Focus stays in the terminal; the new space becomes the landing spot. Split is refused only at the depth or tile limit.
Grow ← ↑ → ↓Grows into empty space on that side: the nearest split on that axis where this tile sits on the opposite side and the far side is all empty. Refusals: "At the left edge", "Occupied tile above"…
Empty spaceRight-click only (spaces stay unfocusable). Split carves the space; New shell here starts a shell in it; Close space gives its area to its neighbours, like dragging a gutter shut.
